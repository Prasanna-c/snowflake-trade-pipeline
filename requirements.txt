# ---- Transitive, pinned on purpose ----
# sqlfluff pulls chardet through diff-cover and does not care which version. The Snowflake
# connector does: its vendored copy of requests accepts chardet >=3.0.2,<6, and anything outside
# that range makes it print "urllib3 (...) or chardet (...)/charset_normalizer (...) doesn't match a
# supported version" before the output of every command in the project. Nothing is broken by it --
# the connector prefers charset_normalizer anyway -- but a banner on every dbt run, every load and
# every query trains a reader to skip the first two lines of output, and the day one of those lines
# matters, it gets skipped too.
chardet==5.2.0
